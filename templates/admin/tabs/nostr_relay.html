Built-in Nostr Relay (Web of Trust)
Serves NIP-01 in its own thread at /relay on the port below. Only accepts/syncs notes from a web of trust. Front it with TLS (e.g. wss://relay.yourdomain/). Restart the service after enabling.
Internal port the relay binds (default 3052). Your reverse proxy upgrades wss://relay.yourdomain/ → 127.0.0.1:<port>/relay.
The relay's own sync/outbox/WoT traffic skips the built-in Tor proxy — faster and avoids the proxy-startup error spam. Doesn't affect the bots. Restart after changing.
Default 127.0.0.1 (front with nginx). Use 0.0.0.0 only if exposing directly.
