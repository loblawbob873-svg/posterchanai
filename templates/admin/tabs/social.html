Fediverse server (ActivityPub)

This node is a fediverse server: Mastodon, Pleroma, Akkoma, Misskey, GoToSocial and Lemmy can find, follow, reply to, react to and message its users, and they can follow fediverse accounts back. Nothing is kept in a separate database — what arrives is stored as Nostr events, and members' own Nostr posts are sent out as they are published. Takes effect without a restart.

Every local user with a name on this node (the NIP-05 names on the Relay tab) is @name@your domain.
Where /.well-known/webfinger answers. Leave blank to use the NIP-05 domain, so name@domain on Nostr and @name@domain on the fediverse are one identity. Changing it later breaks existing follows.
Not only local users: anyone on Nostr this relay holds a profile for gets a readable handle (@dana_4b56@your domain). Fediverse people can follow, mention and reply to them — delivered to their own relays — and their replies, reactions and boosts of fediverse posts go out.
Fediverse direct messages arrive as encrypted Nostr DMs, and Nostr DMs to fediverse accounts are delivered to them. This server reads these messages as it carries them — any bridge between two encryption systems does. Anyone who is not blocked can write; a user who mutes a fediverse account (their Nostr mute list) stops its messages.
Blocking
Nothing from these reaches this server and nothing is sent to them. An instance line covers its subdomains and both spellings of an internationalised name; user@host (or a profile link) blocks that one account. The Relay tab's Blocked bridges/relays list is honoured too, and blocking a fediverse person's npub on the relay (or from the client) blocks them here.
Advanced
Servers whose public name resolves to a private address from here (split DNS — hosted behind the same router). They are otherwise refused, because this server never fetches private addresses for strangers.
Off by default. Fediverse posts stored here stay on this relay. Turn on to let them federate to the wider Nostr network.
