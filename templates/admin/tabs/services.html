Nostr ↔ Fediverse Bridge
Mirror the fediverse global timeline into this instance's Nostr global timeline. Each fedi author becomes a Nostr "puppet" account with a NIP-05 here; replies and quote-posts are preserved. One shared read account (below) feeds the mirror for everyone — it never posts on anyone's behalf. To interact (reply/like/repost) or receive personal DMs/notifications, each user links their own Pleroma account in User Settings → Social and opts in there (and must be a NIP-05 user on this instance). Restart after changing.
